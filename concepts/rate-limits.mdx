Two kinds of limit apply to every send:

- **Send limits** cap how fast an inbox sends, to anyone.
- **Cold outreach cap** limits how many **new recipients** you email per UTC day. Starts low and grows as you build history.

Replies and follow-ups only meet the first. Cold outreach meets both.

## Send limits

| Limit | Value | Scope |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Burst | 10/minute | Per inbox |
| Daily | 200/day | Per inbox |
| Inbox creation | 100/day | Per account |

Over one of these, the API returns `429` with a `Retry-After` header:

```json
{
  "error": "rate_limit_exceeded",
  "message": "Max 10 sends per minute per inbox"
}
```

## Cold outreach cap

A recipient is **new** the first time an inbox emails an address that has never written to it. Each To/Cc address counts once; Bcc, your own inboxes, and anyone who has already emailed or been emailed by that inbox don't count. Warmth is per inbox, so a contact known to inbox A is still new to inbox B.

## Check your limits

`GET /v1/limits/cold` returns today's caps before you hit them: the account cap and what you've used, every verified domain's tier and what unlocks the next one, and a `nextStep` sentence your agent can act on. Nothing is counted or changed by calling it.

```json
{
  "mode": "reputation",
  "resetsAt": "2026-09-30T00:00:00.000Z",
  "reputationState": "good",
  "account": {
    "limit": 150,
    "usedToday": 37,
    "remaining": 113,
    "ceiling": 500,
    "avgNewPerDay7d": 31.4,
    "replyRate": 0.168,
    "sustainPerDayToGrow": 76
  },
  "domains": [
    {
      "domain": "mail.acme.com",
      "tier": 2,
      "limit": 100,
      "usedToday": 17,
      "nextTier": { "limit": null, "minAgeDays": 28, "minLifetimeFirstContacts": 400, "daysToGo": 0, "contactsToGo": 61 }
    }
  ],
  "nextStep": "You've emailed 37 of 150 new recipients today. Your 7-day average is 31.4/day; sustain more than 76/day and the cap rises (up to 500/day). ..."
}
```

`sustainPerDayToGrow` is the number to plan around: average more than that many new recipients a day over a week, keep bounces and complaints clean, and the account cap rises. A `nextTier.limit` of `null` means the domain graduates to the account cap. An inbox-scoped key sees its own inbox and domain; pass `?inboxId=` with an account key to get the same view for one inbox. The API reference lists every field.

The same numbers appear on the [Domains page](https://console.openmail.sh/domains) in the console.

## When you get a 429

```json
{
  "error": "cold_outreach_limit",
  "message": "This sending domain has reached today's limit of new recipients. The limit rises as the domain ages and builds a clean sending history. Try again after midnight UTC.",
  "scope": "domain",
  "limit": 20,
  "resetsAt": "2026-09-30T00:00:00.000Z",
  "nextStep": "This domain is on a warm-up tier of 20 new recipients/day. It moves to 50/day once it is 7+ days old and has reached 50+ first contacts. Check GET /v1/limits/cold for where it stands."
}
```

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the scope">
    `inbox`, `domain` or `customer` (your account). It tells you which cap you hit, so you know whether another inbox or domain would help. On a `domain` block, another inbox on the same domain won't.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pause cold sends until resetsAt">
    Caps reset at midnight UTC. Retrying earlier fails the same way. Call `GET /v1/limits/cold` to see what's left on your other inboxes and domains, and `nextStep` for what raises this cap.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep replying">
    The rejected send didn't go out and didn't count. Replies and follow-ups to existing contacts are unaffected, so your agents can keep working threads.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reputation

Every inbox has a reputation state: `good`, `watch`, `throttled` or `suspended`. It's computed from the inbox's permanent bounces and spam complaints over the trailing 7 days. Keep bounces under 5% and complaints under 0.1% and the inbox stays in `good`, with full caps.

### Thresholds

| Signal | `watch` | `throttled` | `suspended` |
|--------|---------|-------------|-------------|
| Bounce rate | Any bounce | 5% | 10% |
| Complaint rate | Any complaint | 0.1% | 0.3% |

### What each state does

| State | Effect | How it clears |
|-------|--------|---------------|
| `watch` | Caps stop growing; a custom domain drops one tier | On its own, as the rate falls |
| `throttled` | Daily send limit cut to 25%; cold caps halved | On its own, as the rate falls |
| `suspended` | Every send from the inbox refused | After review by OpenMail; the `inbox.reactivated` [webhook](/pages/webhooks/events) fires |

### If an inbox is suspended

1. Fix what caused the bounces or complaints: validate the list, check DNS, drop the source.
2. Contact us for review.
3. Wait for the `inbox.reactivated` webhook before sending again.

For how to send so you never meet these limits, read [Email deliverability](/best-practices/email-deliverability).
